DETACHED RESIDENTIAL PROPERTY, WITH OUTBUILDINGS, ON C.8 ACRES OF GOOD QUALITY AGRICULTURAL LAND.

Davis Auctioneers bring to the market this traditional farmhouse on C. 8 acres of good quality agricultural lands, situated just 5 minutes from the village of Ballynacarrigy. The property is brilliantly located, just a 20-minute drive to Mullingar, with Ballymahon and Center Parcs Longford Forest Holiday Resort a 10-15 minute drive away.

In need of some care and repair, as well as modernising, the c.175m2 residential accommodation comprises of a sitting room, living room, kitchen-dining room, utility, four bedrooms, a bathroom and a WC (on the ground floor). This impressive holding also incorporates agricultural outbuildings, workshops, as well as a spacious yard and cattle handling facilities.

Vacant Property Refurbishment Grants are available for this type of property subject to the terms and conditions.

Please call to us at 53 Main Street, Longford, or phone us on 043-33 50555 to arrange your viewing.
